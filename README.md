# JESA Best Team Player Portfolio

Evidence-led, presentation-ready portfolio for Vihanga Rathnayake's JESA Best Team Player application.

## Narrative structure

Hero → Identity → Team Pulse → Criteria → Team Stories → Growth → Evidence → Closing.

## Interactive evidence system

- Criteria heatmaps illuminate only the rubric dimensions supported by each story.
- Expandable stories separate the goal, pressure point, support action, adaptation and collective result.
- Role-fluidity maps show how the contribution changed as the team's needs changed.
- The Invisible Work layer contrasts the visible outcome with behind-the-scenes handoffs and gap-filling.
- A Trust Bank and Team Pulse turn reliability and collective movement into memorable visual metaphors.
- Empathy snapshots and a pressure-to-support loop make growth concrete.
- The Team Echo Wall remains locked until permission-approved, specific teammate statements are available.
- Story Path presentation mode provides a ten-minute sequence, prompts, progress ring and pinned evidence.

## Evidence integrity

The site does not publish invented testimonials, continuity percentages or unsupported emotional-support stories. Evidence is marked Available, Strengthen or Must add.

## Remaining applicant actions

- Collect 3-5 permission-approved teammate echoes describing specific observed behaviour.
- Add verified behind-the-scenes photographs with dates, context and action-focused captions.
- Confirm exact dates, outcomes and continuity claims against original records.
- Prepare named referee details for private judge review.
- Rehearse Story Path on the actual interview device and keep a PDF backup.
